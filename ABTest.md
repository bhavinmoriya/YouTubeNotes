**A/B testing** = comparing two versions of something to see which performs better.

**How it works:**
1. Take one thing (a webpage, email, ad, app screen…)
2. Make **Version A** (the original) and **Version B** (with one change — different button color, headline, price, etc.)
3. Show **A to half your users** and **B to the other half** — randomly
4. Measure which version gets more of what you want: clicks, sign-ups, purchases
5. The winner becomes your new default

**Simple example:** An online shop tests two buy buttons:
- 🅰️ "Buy Now" → 100 sales out of 1,000 visitors (10%)
- 🅱️ "Add to Cart" → 130 sales out of 1,000 visitors (13%)

Version B wins — so they switch to "Add to Cart".

**Key rules of thumb:**
- **Change one thing at a time** — otherwise you don't know what caused the difference
- **Split groups randomly** — so they're comparable
- **Wait long enough / get enough people** — a few lucky clicks don't count
- **Measure with real data, not opinions** — let the numbers decide

In short: it's a fair, controlled contest between two options where the users' behavior picks the winner.
